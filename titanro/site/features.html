What's new on TitanRO

The season server at play.titanro.net is a rebuild of TitanRO from the client up: two clients with one sign-in, jRO mechanics, the tools players used to download now built in, and game servers that update without kicking everyone. This page lists what is in the game today and what is still being built.

Play anywhere

One TitanRO account, two clients, and no game passwords.

Two clients, one world

Play on the Windows client or in the browser at play.titanro.net. Both connect to the same servers, so friends can play together on either.

Play anywhere you are, on mobile or desktop: the web client runs in phone and tablet browsers too.

No passwords

Sign in once with Discord, an emailed code or a passkey. The launcher (or the web client's start screen) shows all your characters. Pick one and you're in game, with no login or character-select screens.

Each login uses a single-use token that expires within two minutes, so there is no game password to steal or reuse.

One master account

Your TitanRO account holds all your characters, your language, and your helper settings, which carry over between the Windows and web clients. The same sign-in opens the site pages: market, database, MVP timers, WoE tracker and WoE stills.

Up to two characters online at once per account, on any mix of clients.

Fast launch

We rewrote the Windows client's startup. Game connections now open while the client loads, and the client no longer checks its game data for old files at every start.

Discord presence

jRO mechanics & dungeons

The game follows Japan's official server (jRO) as of 2021-12-31: skills, skill trees, level caps, exp tables and stat formulas.

jRO 2021 mechanics

Skills, skill trees, max skill levels, cast times and descriptions follow jRO at our pinned date, including the Evolution update. Base level goes to 185 and job level to 65 (Doram 55), with jRO's exp tables. Automated tests compare skills, items and formulas against a reference implementation on thousands of cases, and every intended difference is written down with its source.

Every stat point counts. Stats add to their values point by point instead of in steps of 10, and most stats feed several values, so balanced builds are viable alongside all-in ones:

We're adding the dungeons and instances jRO had by 2021-12-31. Live now:

  • Glast Heim Abyss and Nogue Road 3F, with jRO's six weekly Terror quests and essences
  • Memorial dungeons such as Orc's Memory, Last Room and the Shrine of Demon God
  • Illusion dungeons: Frozen, Moonlight, Vampire, Turtle Island, Teddy Bear, Luanda, Labyrinth

Rifts: generated maps

Rifts are new maps put together from pieces of existing dungeons such as the Culvert and Glast Heim. Both clients build the map from its name, so there's nothing to download. Each rift has monsters, a boss and a boss chest, and closes an hour after it opens or a minute after everyone has left.

Open to everyone through the Rift Warden in Prontera while we tune them.

War of Emperium

Castle stills

When an Emperium breaks, and again when WoE ends, the server records a still of the whole castle: every player, monster and NPC with their facing and status effects, every ground skill, and the guilds present. After WoE, anyone signed in can open the still and walk around it in 3D. Hidden players and invisible traps show only to the viewer's own guild and its allies.

Open the sample still (no sign-in needed). It's a staged test WoE, not a real one.

WoE tracker

Castle owners, breaks and guild kills per WoE on woe.html and in an in-game panel.

One character per account in castles

Only one character from each TitanRO account can be inside a WoE castle at a time.

Security & community

Encrypted connections

The clients' connections to our servers are encrypted (TLS):

  • game traffic from both clients, starting with the first login packet: chat, trades, market searches and everything else
  • the Windows client's web services (settings, guild emblems, party board)
  • sign-in and the launcher's downloads and updates

The unencrypted game ports are closed.

Nothing hidden to reveal

In PvP and WoE, the server doesn't send enemies anything the game hides from them, such as hidden characters and invisible traps. A modified client has nothing to reveal.

Account-wide blocking

/ex name blocks the whole account behind that character, not just one character: chat, whispers, invitations and trade requests from any of its characters stop. @blocklist and @unblock manage the list.

Reports and chat sanctions

@report files a report with the evidence attached. Reports about one account are grouped into one case. Public chat sanctions go 1 day, then 3 days, then permanent; whispers, party and guild chat stay open, and 90 clean days reset the count.

Smart patcher

Downloads only what changed

The launcher compares your files with the release and fetches only the changed parts, compressed and often as small binary diffs.

We removed game data nobody can reach (unused maps, art for items that can't be obtained, and more):

Missing files come back by themselves

If the Windows client asks for a map, model, sprite, texture or sound it doesn't have, it gets the file on the spot and reports it. A nightly job adds every reported file back into the release for everyone.

Server architecture

Several map servers

The world is split across three map servers (Rune-Midgarts, Schwarzwald and Arunafeltz, and the rest) that share players' load. Whispers, parties, guilds, friends, channels and GM commands work across all of them.

Shared towns and ghosts

Busy maps can run on two servers at once (Prontera and some of its fields for now). Players on the other copy show up as translucent "ghosts" you can see walking and sitting but not interact with.

Updates without kicking everyone

Old and new server builds work side by side, so an update restarts one server at a time instead of all at once. Players on a shared map move to its other copy when their server restarts.

Guild Wars-style rollouts

After a non-critical update, you can stay on your current map server for up to 24 hours. When you change maps, you land on the newest build of the target map. WoE castles always run the newest build.

The servers for this are built and switched off until it's ready.

Map servers in other regions

Because maps can sit on any map server, a region's maps could run closer to its players, for example a Prontera WoE hosted in North America and a Geffen WoE in Southeast Asia. All map servers run in one data center today.

Languages

English, Portuguese (Brazil) and Korean

Both clients use UTF-8, so chat and character, party and guild names can use accents and Hangul. Pick your language once on your TitanRO account. New translations ship without a server update and load the next time the map servers start.

Planned

Account friends

Friends between TitanRO accounts, separate from in-game character friends. An account friend sees whether you're online on any character without seeing which characters are yours. Messages sent while you're offline are waiting when you log in.
