Skill simulator

Each job's skill points are its job levels less one, counted as if you changed jobs at the job's top level. The server makes you spend a job's points before you learn the next job's skills. Raising a skill also raises what it needs; lowering one unlearns the skills that need it. Quest skills (marked) cost no points. Shift-click to go straight to the top or to 0.
